# Asset Sources — Official Isuzu Drive Refresh

Primary vehicle visuals are now derived from the user-shared Google Drive folder:
`All Unit Foto Isuzu EURO4` inside the Isuzu media library.

The original PNG/JPEG masters are large official media assets. For production, they are resized to a maximum of ~2600 px and exported as transparent/optimized WebP files in `public/images/isuzu/models/`.
