V14 — Model-level product catalog

- /produk now exposes model-level names matching the 12 Isuzu truck models currently listed by Oto: Traga, ELF NLR/NMR/NPS/NQR/Microbus, GIGA FRR/FTR/FVR/FVM/FVZ/GXZ.
- D-MAX and MU-X remain in the catalog because the Astra Isuzu Yogyakarta site covers more than Oto's truck-only page.
- Homepage remains intentionally grouped into five broad families (Traga, ELF, GIGA, D-MAX, MU-X).
- Family detail pages /produk/isuzu-elf and /produk/isuzu-giga remain available, preserving all 47 Astra variants already collected, including GIGA GVR/GVZ variants that Oto does not expose as separate model cards.
- Model detail pages reuse Astra variant/spec data from the existing project dataset.
- Version bumped to 0.9.0.
